Overview

Geometry, force, and speed in one curved fingertip

FasTac combines a compact RGB-NIR curved fingertip, high-fidelity geometry reconstruction, position-aware three-axis force estimation, and an FPGA image-to-normal-force pipeline. The system is designed for dexterous-hand integration and dynamic contact perception.

System and Method

End-to-end tactile sensing research and development

FasTac spans sensor structure and imaging hardware, geometric and force calibration, perception algorithms, and edge acceleration.

01

Sensor structure and synchronous RGB-NIR imaging

A custom miniature camera module uses an OV2736 RGB-IR CMOS sensor to capture visible and near-infrared information from the same optical center. The compact curved sensing surface is shaped for fingertip integration and broad contact coverage.

02

Automated acquisition and calibration

A CNC platform synchronizes tactile images, contact poses, and six-axis force measurements. CAD alignment and rendering provide surface-normal and depth ground truth for geometric calibration and model training.

03

3D geometry and HyperForce

Coordinate-aware RGB-NIR normal inference and boundary-prior Poisson integration reconstruct curved contact geometry. HyperForce then combines normal displacement, marker-derived tangential displacement, and spatial encoding to estimate three-axis force.

Edge Deployment

FPGA image-to-normal-force pipeline

The PS-PL architecture performs a complete tactile decoding path rather than accelerating a single network. It preserves an explicit distinction between measured accuracy, scheduled latency, and Vivado-estimated energy.

Results

High-fidelity reconstruction and force perception

Experiments evaluate curved-surface geometry, three-axis force accuracy, edge latency, and continuous high-frequency decoding.

Multi-object 3D reconstruction

Fine geometry is reconstructed for a strawberry, Type-C connector, bottle cap, fingertip, and LED array. RGB-NIR sensing improves normal recovery in difficult curved regions.

Three-axis force estimation

Component-wise NMAE reaches 2.37%, 2.41%, and 2.74% for Fx, Fy, and Fz, respectively.

High-frequency tactile decoding

The FPGA pipeline preserves the 100 Hz signal while CPU and GPU outputs alias beyond their continuous measurement bandwidth.
